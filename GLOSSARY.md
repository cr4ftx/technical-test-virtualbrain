# PokeBattle

A web app to browse the Pokédex and battle Pokémon against each other.

## Language

**Pokémon**:
One creature of the Pokédex. Always accented, and the plural is also "Pokémon".
_Avoid_: Pokemon, Pokemons, Pokémons

**Pokédex**:
The full set of Pokémon the app knows about (generations 1–8).
_Avoid_: Pokedex
